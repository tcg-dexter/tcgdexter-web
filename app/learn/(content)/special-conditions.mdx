# Special Conditions and checkup

Some attacks don't just deal damage — they leave the defending Pokémon Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, Paralyzed or Confused. These are **Special Conditions**, and they're where most beginner rules arguments come from.

Two facts cover almost everything:

<Callout kind="rule">
Special Conditions only affect the **Active Pokémon**, and they **all disappear the moment that Pokémon leaves the Active Spot** — retreated, switched, dragged out by your opponent, or promoted after a Knock Out. Evolving clears them too.
</Callout>

That second half is the escape hatch. Any condition, however nasty, is one retreat away from gone.

## The five conditions

### Poisoned

<Card id="sv5-103" />

Between turns, a Poisoned Pokémon takes **1 damage counter — 10 damage**. It keeps happening every turn until it's cured or leaves the Active Spot. Poison never wears off by itself.

Haunter's Super Poison Breath does 30 and Poisons. That's really 40 by the time your opponent's next turn starts, and 50 by the one after.

### Burned

Between turns, a Burned Pokémon takes **2 damage counters — 20 damage**. Then flip a coin: **heads and the Burn goes away**, tails and it stays.

### Asleep

<Card id="sv6-43" />

An Asleep Pokémon **cannot attack and cannot retreat**. Between turns, flip a coin: **heads and it wakes up**, tails and it stays asleep.

Sleep is the harshest of the five, because the escape hatch is partly closed — you can't retreat out of it. You need a Switch-style card, an evolution, or a lucky flip.

### Paralyzed

<Card id="sv6-65" />

A Paralyzed Pokémon **cannot attack and cannot retreat**. No coin flip: it's **removed at the checkup after the affected player's own turn**. So Paralysis costs its victim exactly one turn — but that's often the turn that decides the game.

### Confused

A Confused Pokémon **can** retreat, and **can** attack — but when it attacks, flip a coin first. **Tails and the attack fails**, and the Pokémon puts **30 damage on itself** instead.

## They stack — but only some of them

<Callout kind="gotcha">
**Asleep, Paralyzed and Confused replace each other** — a Pokémon can only have one of the three. Getting Confused while Asleep wakes it up and Confuses it instead.

**Poisoned and Burned are separate**, and coexist with anything. A Pokémon can be Poisoned, Burned *and* Asleep all at once, taking 30 between turns and unable to act.
</Callout>

## Pokémon Checkup

Between every turn there's a step called the **Pokémon Checkup**, where all of this resolves. It happens after one player's turn ends and before the next player's begins, and it applies to **both** players' Active Pokémon.

The order:

1. **Poison** — 10 damage.
2. **Burn** — 20 damage, then flip; heads removes it.
3. **Asleep** — flip; heads wakes up.
4. **Paralyzed** — removed, if the affected player has now had their turn.

Knock Outs from checkup damage are real Knock Outs: Prize cards get taken, and the player who lost the Pokémon promotes a new Active from their Bench.
